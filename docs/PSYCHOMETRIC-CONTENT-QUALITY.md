# Psychometric practice

The student page has three compact keyboard-accessible tabs: Clinical, Situational and Readiness. It removes simulator marketing, duplicate titles, invented exam durations and large introductory cards. Each visited tab keeps its current response while switching tabs; assignments and the existing no-repeat policy remain independent per student and area.

New RAG Clinical Judgment drafts use one focused clinical decision, four distinct plausible options, one supported best answer and a separate source-grounded rationale for every option. They should test interpretation, prioritization, the next appropriate action or reassessment, rather than definitions. Scope screening and evidence checks still apply, and sources without enough decision-making evidence may yield no activity.

New Situational Judgment drafts require realistic trade-offs around safety, communication, teamwork, consent, escalation or accountability, with four distinct responses and explanations for all alternatives. Local laws and policies must not be invented.

New Readiness drafts remain educational reflection. Prompts ask for a preparation gap, a response, an improvement action and a review plan; at least three distinct observable rubric criteria are required. Feedback is rubric-based and provisional or awaiting review, not a validated psychological assessment or licensing score.

New source-grounded MCQs retain their answer and explanations privately. The API varies option order reproducibly by student and activity. Explanations refer to option text rather than original letters, so shuffling cannot invalidate feedback. Existing activities retain their stored formats and are not silently rewritten or republished. Generate new reviewed batches to populate the improved formats.

## Licensing information

The third-party example at https://www.neelim.com/exam-prep/paramedic states SCFHS 40% and QCHP 50%. Those figures were not independently verified against current official paramedic/EMT-category documents in this session: attempted SCFHS document paths returned generic HTML, and the Qatar DHP qualifying examination page timed out. They are therefore not embedded as verified platform requirements. The collapsed exam-information area links to SCFHS and Qatar DHP and tells learners to check their precise professional category and blueprint. The three practice areas are a MedProHub learning structure, not a claim that every licensing exam has these three sections. Requirements for other tracks, including Nursing, must not be inferred from a paramedic preparation page.

To populate the improved formats, use Content Studio → Books & learning resources → an approved indexed source → Psychometric areas. Select substantive clinical/communication chapters, preview a bounded run, generate, then review questions, all four explanations and evidence before publication. Readiness batches require material about professional preparation and reflective improvement. Definition-only passages cannot be forced into a rich assessment and may yield no draft. Existing question-bank format controls do not determine the new psychometric formats.
