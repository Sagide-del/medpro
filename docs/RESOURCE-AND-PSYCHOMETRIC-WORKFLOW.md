# Resource and psychometric content

Use Content Studio → Books & learning resources. Upload a resource, review its classification, approve and index it, then choose its chapters/page range and student tracks.

The default outputs are Cheat Sheets, Drug Reference, Study Guides, Clinical Judgment, Situational Judgment and Readiness Reflection. Use the Revision resources / Psychometric areas / Both presets to choose the destination group. Question Bank and the other retained outputs remain optional. Preview the workload, queue generation, review the source-linked drafts and publish approved items. Each run remains capped at 200 passage/track/destination batches: use smaller chapter/page ranges for large books.

Every generated artifact must retain its source edition, PDF page and evidence quote. Drug drafts require explicit medication evidence; no doses are inferred. Situational and readiness tasks require relevant source evidence and can legitimately produce no artifact. Readiness is educational reflection, not a validated psychological test or diagnosis. Nothing is automatically published. Institution-private sources remain draft-only.

Clinical Case Scenarios is retired as a separate page; Clinical Judgment remains a core area of Psychometric Test. Clinical Protocols and Skills Videos are retired from navigation, resource cards and new generation/publication controls. Their records are retained. Old student links redirect to Psychometric Test or Study Guides; retired resource feeds return 410.

## Fresh psychometric practice

Each student receives one current published activity per area in their enrolled track. Assignments are persisted, so reloads, signing out/in and concurrent requests resume the same unfinished task. After submission, Next activity selects a new prompt. Previously assigned and submitted prompts are excluded across the three areas, including exact and close text duplicates (word-set Jaccard similarity ≥ 0.72). Historical responses from before this change also count as prior exposure.

New assignments favor activities assigned to fewer students, with random tie breaking. Different students receive varied activities when the available pool permits; questions are not globally reserved to one student. A finite bank cannot guarantee every student receives a unique test, and lexical duplicate checks cannot detect every semantic paraphrase. When a student exhausts eligible practice, the system waits for more approved content instead of silently recycling it.

Only the assigned task can be submitted; completed tasks cannot be resubmitted as fresh practice. Original submission tokens remain idempotent. Public activity payloads exclude answers, rubrics and evidence metadata. Written feedback remains provisional or awaits review as before.

This assignment policy applies to the three Psychometric Test areas. Shared study references, sequential Kenya EMS cases, teacher assignments and formal MCQ exams keep their existing access/review policies.
